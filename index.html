Hardware Matrix

VilotNI 1.5 Architecture

A browser-native WebGPU architecture executing 3,000,000 sparse neurons with residual skip connections and autonomous self-evaluating reinforcement learning.

WebGPU Compute Shaders

Bypasses CPU bottlenecks via WGSL compute passes. Dispatches 782 parallel workgroups per layer directly to your hardware GPU.

Residual Skip Strides

Each of the 15 hidden steps adds its previous state back into the new activation vector, preserving energy and preventing gradient vanishing across 3 million neurons.

Autonomous RSL Engine

Evaluates vocabulary entropy and sequence diversity on every reply, rewarding confident pathways and pruning repetitive loops without requiring human intervention.

Dynamic Dictionary Mapping

Zero hardcoded tokens. Ingests any custom dictionary file into dynamic allocation buffers on launch with adaptive statistical confidence scoring.
